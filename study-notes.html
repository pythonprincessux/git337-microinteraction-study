4. Motion and CSS explanation

Transition 1: The .action transform transitions for about .15s when the user hovers over making a small lift or press movement that makes the interaction more clear.

Transition 2: The .action box-shadow transitions for about .15s during hover and active states, changing the control to reinforce the lift and pressed feedback.

Keyframe animation: The reveal confirmation animation changes the opacity from 0 to 1 and the transform from translateY(-.25rem) to translateY(0). It runs once for .25s with ease out when the details element opens, helping draw attention to the confirmation message for the user and for clarity.

Why these properties: Transform and opacity give the page movement without changing the document layout, while the box shadow changes the visual depth of the action control. These choices keep the motion short, brief, and focused on interaction feedback.

Reduced-motion alternative: When reduced motion is preferred, the action transition and confirmation animation are not there anymore. Hover, focus, active, checked, and open states still give the page visible feedback without movement.
